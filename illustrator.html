The world

A World Between Light and Shadow

Dream of The White Wolf, The Devin Spade Saga is a planned trilogy of fantasy novels exploring fate, choice, duality, and the struggle between light and shadow.

Who will you choose to be when fate has already decided?

The saga unfolds in a world shaped by ancient myths, mysterious forces, and the choices its inhabitants make. It draws on the natural world, spiritual questions, and the complexities of the inner self.

I'm looking for an artist who can translate that vision into imagery. Artwork that feels mythic without losing its humanity. Mysterious, atmospheric, and rich with meaning.

Explore the saga
The creative opportunity

An Opportunity to Create

The first book, A Wolf on Fire, is complete. I'm preparing it for publication and beginning to shape the visual identity of the saga.

Book cover design is the immediate priority. Beyond that, I'd like to explore the possibilities for a broader artistic collaboration.

Depending on the artist, the creative approach, and what makes sense for both of us, the work might include:

  • Book cover illustration and design.
  • Character portraits and promotional artwork.
  • Illustrations for the website and social media.
  • A consistent visual identity across the trilogy.
  • Additional artwork for future editions.

I'm open to different artistic mediums, creative approaches, and ways of working together. The goal is to find the right fit, then determine what we can create together.

The author

The Author Behind the Saga

I'm Michael Stryder, an independent author bringing Dream of The White Wolf from manuscript to publication.

My background spans photography, marketing, advertising, and communications. Those experiences have shaped how I approach storytelling, visual identity, and the larger publishing vision.

The first manuscript is complete. The website is live. The next stage is bringing the world to readers.

I'm building this independently, one deliberate step at a time, with the ambition to create a lasting body of work around the saga.

An honest beginning

Let's Explore What's Possible

I'm an independent author funding this project myself, and my current budget for illustration is limited.

I want to be transparent about that from the beginning.

I haven't predetermined the scope of the commission or the arrangement. I'm open to discussing creative approaches, project phases, rates, and potential funding options once we have a clearer understanding of the work involved.

If the right collaboration requires additional funding, I would consider organizing a fundraiser around a defined project and goal.

I'm not expecting anyone to work for free. I'm looking for an opportunity to explore what we might create together, what it would take to do it well, and whether there's a practical path forward for both of us.
