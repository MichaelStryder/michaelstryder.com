World · The Way

Every wolf walks a path.

Every wolf lives by The Way, whether they follow its teachings, uphold its laws, or choose a path of their own. It shapes the world they inherit, the lives they lead, and the choices that define them.

Yet the world is older than any wolf's understanding of it. Beneath familiar lands and ancient forests, beyond the reach of ordinary sight, lie mysteries that have endured for generations. Some are preserved in stories. Others survive only in dreams, waiting for the day they are remembered.

Here, light and shadow reach beyond the world that can be seen. Every journey leads somewhere unexpected, and every wolf carries more than what the world can see.

This is a world of ancient truths, forgotten histories, and paths that do not always lead where they seem. To understand it, one must first learn The Way.

Those who walk The Way

The wolves of the saga

Each wolf carries a history of their own. Their paths cross in a world where loyalty is tested, old truths linger, and no journey leaves its travelers unchanged.

Stryder

Stryder is a white wolf who has spent much of his life overlooked by his own kind. A follower of The Way, he leaves his home and the family he loves to answer a call he can no longer ignore.

As he journeys into a world far larger than the one he knew, Stryder finds companionship, discovers truths that challenge everything he believes, and begins to understand what it means to follow The Way when all paths lead to Shadow.

Kalyn

Kalyn is a striped black and grey wolf who lost his den to the shadow wolves. A skilled fighter and negotiator, he is the last survivor of his pack and has since become Stryder's closest companion and right hand.

Loyal and decisive, Kalyn helps hold the pack together as they venture into an unfamiliar world. Having lost his first pack, he is determined to protect the one he has found.

Amar

Amar is a silver wolf with sapphire eyes and a longing for a life beyond the walls of her childhood den. When she discovers that the mother she trusted has been keeping her from the world, she leaves home to find the adventure she has always dreamed of.

With a gift for bringing others together, Amar becomes an essential part of the pack. As they venture into unfamiliar lands, she helps open doors that might otherwise remain closed, while finding a place among companions who have become her family.

Lokan

Lokan is a towering, scar-marked wolf with sable fur, amber eyes, and a scent of smoke and spice. A survivor who knows the world's less-traveled paths, he has a knack for finding whatever the pack needs, often through connections best left unexplained.

With Lokan, there is always a price, though it may not be the one you expect. He travels by his own rules, keeps his own counsel, and rarely reveals everything he knows. He offers the pack a way through a world where the usual rules no longer apply.

Vyrex

Vyrex is a massive wolf clad in black hide armor, his scarred body bearing the marks of a lifetime of battle. His fangs are worn with age, yet sharpened by use. Few creatures have faced the monsters he has defeated, and fewer still would willingly stand in his path.

Born and bred for war, Vyrex has spent his life fighting battles few could survive. Now the Champion of Hollowrest, he is a legend forged in blood and steel, feared for the force he brings to the battlefield.

Sylas

Sylas is a sleek black wolf with a grey muzzle and emerald eyes. Wise beyond the measure of most wolves, she has spent her long life learning from the world around her. She knows the language of roots and growing things, and carries an understanding of the natural world few can match.

A natural leader, Sylas guides others with patience and quiet authority. Though she has never had a pack of her own, she knows wolves across the land. When she joins the journey, she brings the wisdom of age and a steady presence to those finding their way through an unfamiliar world.

Wisp

Wisp is a small, brown wolf with violet eyes and an ever-present bundle of scrolls, ink, and quills. Curious and meticulous, she records what others overlook and asks the questions few think to ask. She has learned that every story has more than one side, and she is determined to uncover the whole truth.

Traveling alongside her twin brother, Crag, and the wolves they meet along the way, Wisp seeks answers to questions that reach far beyond the written page. She keeps a record of their journey, determined that no detail, however small, should be lost to time.

Crag

Crag is a large, brown wolf with a keen mind and a talent for understanding how things work. More comfortable studying a problem than joining a game, he has spent much of his life observing, experimenting, and searching for practical solutions. He has a knack for finding uses for things others might discard.

Traveling with his twin sister, Wisp, Crag brings a careful eye and a steady presence to the journey. When others lose their way, he looks for what has gone wrong and how to make it right. Some problems, however, are harder to repair than others.

Reyven

Reyven is a lean, strong wolf with rust and black stripes, sharp features, and eyes that are always searching the distance. Raised in a hunting den, he became an exceptional hunter and marksman, skilled at finding paths and possibilities others overlook. He prefers precision to brute force, and rarely takes a shot without good reason.

An old friend of Thane's, Reyven has known a life shaped by hunting, hard choices, and service under The Order. As the world he once knew falls into shadow, he must find a new path forward, one that offers more than another battle to fight.

Thane

Thane is a white wolf with a strong, battle-trained frame, silver armor, and the bearing of a noble raised to lead. Heir to the throne of Cyndralis, he lost everything after his childhood friend betrayed him. Now he carries his broadsword into a world that has left him with little to call his own.

Trained in the traditions of knighthood, Thane holds fast to honor even as the world around him descends into shadow. With his old friend Reyven at his side, he faces an uncertain future and a past he has yet to reckon with.

Saga · The books

The Devin Spade Saga

A dark fairy tale wrapped in a fantasy adventure, following wolves through a world of ancient mysteries, difficult choices, and shadows that reach farther than they seem.

Book One

A Wolf on Fire

Some calls cannot be ignored.

When Stryder leaves the only home he has ever known, he enters a world of ancient mysteries, shifting loyalties, and shadows that reach farther than he could have imagined. Along the way, he finds companions as different from one another as the paths that brought them together.

Among them is Vyrex, a warrior whose strength has been forged through a lifetime of battle. But even the mightiest wolf must discover what strength alone cannot overcome.

Book Two

The Chosen Wolf

To be chosen is one thing. To choose is another.

As Amar and Kalyn discover what it means to belong to one another, their journey carries them beyond familiar shores into a world built on chains, where power determines who is free and who must serve. The struggle ahead will test the bonds between them, and the beliefs upon which they stand.

But in a world where every choice has a cost, love may demand more than either of them expected.

Book Three

The Two Wolves

Every story has a past. But who remembers it?

As the boundary between the world and the spirit realm begins to unravel, the wolves must confront the uncertainty of memory, the shifting nature of truth, and questions that reach beyond the world they know. Some mysteries change everything. Others change the one who seeks the answer.

Deeper · Beyond the veil

Questions beneath the story

The saga is an adventure, but its questions reach beyond the world its wolves inhabit. It explores the self, the social contract, and the horror of existence through fable, myth, and the lives of those who must choose their way forward.

The Nature of Duality

We often describe the world through opposites: light and shadow, strength and weakness, order and chaos. Yet these labels can conceal as much as they reveal. The same force can sustain life or threaten it, depending on its purpose, its measure, and the circumstances in which it is used. Recognizing complexity does not mean abandoning judgment. It means looking closer before deciding what we see.

The Pattern Repeats

The struggles within a single wolf echo through packs, societies, and the larger questions of existence. The individual shapes the collective, and the collective shapes the individual. The pattern repeats at different scales, never quite the same, but carrying familiar tensions wherever it appears.

The Illusion of a Final Answer

We need systems, principles, and shared rules to live together. But no system can anticipate every circumstance, and no principle removes the need for judgment. Balance is not a final state we reach and keep. It is an ongoing adjustment, made fragile by change and by the certainty that we have found the answer once and for all.

Fate and Choice

Some things are chosen for us. We inherit a body, a history, a world already in motion. We cannot always change what has happened or control what comes next, but we still face choices about how we respond and who we become.

Who will you choose to be when fate has already decided?

Choice is the last thing fate cannot take.

An Ongoing Conversation

I am not writing because I have solved these problems. I am writing because they matter, because the answers are rarely simple, and because I believe we should be able to examine them honestly.

I choose to frame the conversation through fables, through adventure, and through characters we can all recognize and understand. Perhaps, by exploring these questions through their lives, we can approach them more openly than we might through the seemingly incessant noise of the talking heads.

This is not an invitation to agree with me. It is an invitation to look closer, question what seems certain, and consider what may be missing from the conversation.

And it's a wild ride.

This story belongs to its wolves. The questions belong to all of us.

The Twelve Steps to The Way of The White Wolf grew from these same questions. It pairs each step with a parable and practical ways to work with the lesson in everyday life.

The Twelve Steps to The Way of The White Wolf

A practice for recognizing the forces within us, learning how to work with them, and making more conscious choices about the paths we take.

Each step is explored through a parable, followed by practical ways to put its lesson to work. This is not a promise of perfection or a route to a final state of balance. The steps can be revisited as life changes and new challenges emerge. The journey is recursive, because the work of living never truly ends.

The steps make no claim to define the self or resolve the nature of existence. They offer a framework for reflection, a story to consider, and a practice to try.

Hear the Call

Recognize the yearning within.

Admit that your current path is unbalanced, whether by feeding the darkness too much or neglecting the light.

Acknowledge the dual nature of your inner wolf and the need to honor both sides to achieve harmony.

Face the Reflection

Confront your truth with courage.

Reflect on your past choices, behaviors, and patterns.

Identify moments where you fed the darkness and neglected your light, and accept responsibility without judgment.

Seek the Wisdom of the Pack

Learn from others who have walked similar paths.

Open yourself to guidance and wisdom from those who embody balance.

Build a community or pack of like-minded individuals who encourage growth.

Choose the Path of Light

Commit to nurturing the light within.

Make a conscious decision to prioritize behaviors, relationships, and habits that align with your higher self.

Create a personal code of ethics to guide your journey.

Map the Shadows

Explore and understand your darkness.

Identify the fears, anger, and pain that have shaped your shadow wolf.

Delve into the root causes of destructive tendencies without succumbing to self-loathing.

Make Amends with Yourself

Heal your relationship with your inner wolves.

Forgive yourself for past mistakes and recognize the lessons within them.

Develop rituals or practices, such as journaling, meditation, or ceremonies, to honor both the light and dark wolves as parts of your experience.

Speak Your Truth

Express your journey honestly and authentically.

Share your story with someone you trust or through creative outlets like writing, art, or music.

Use your experiences to inspire and connect with others.

Cultivate the Light

Nurture your light wolf daily.

Practice self-care, gratitude, and mindfulness.

Pursue activities and relationships that inspire joy, creativity, and a sense of purpose.

Tame the Shadow Wolf

Harness your darkness into a source of strength.

Channel difficult emotions into constructive action, such as volunteering, physical activity, or creative projects.

Learn to live with and redirect these parts of yourself. The aim is not to erase them, but to keep them from taking the lead when they are not serving a useful purpose.

Strengthen the Pack

Give back to others with humility and love.

Serve your community in ways that align with your values.

Mentor others who are struggling, embodying the balance of light and shadow as a role model.

Seek the White Wolf

Align with your highest self.

Pursue practices that deepen your connection with your purpose and spirituality.

Strive for balance and integration, allowing light and shadow to coexist without either claiming permanent rule.

Walk The Way

Live the teachings in every aspect of your life.

Commit to continuous growth and self-awareness.

Embrace challenges as opportunities to refine your path and inspire others to follow The Way of The White Wolf.

Fabulist · Michael Stryder

Every fable begins somewhere.

Dream of The White Wolf began with a question about forgiveness.

After a painful chapter in my life, I found myself looking back over old wounds and trying to understand what it means to forgive. I began with the parable of the Two Wolves and the idea that the shadow within us is not simply an enemy to defeat. Drawing on the language of Jungian psychology, I started thinking about that shadow as something to understand, live with, and learn to direct.

That reflection became the seed of a personal practice: The Twelve Steps to The Way of The White Wolf. Each step would be paired with a parable from a world of wolves, with The White Wolf and The Shadow Wolf appearing in stories that made the lesson tangible. The steps were meant to offer practical ways to work with the questions, not promises of salvation or a final state of peace.

About a year later, another idea took hold. What if the emotions and forces at work inside us could become characters? Perhaps it would be easier to recognize them if I could watch them speak, clash, make choices, and live with the consequences. The first character I created was Vyrex, the embodiment of rage. Within an hour, Stryder, Kalyn, Amar, and Lokan had joined him. I began writing and let the wolves meet. The story came in bursts, and it took time to understand what it was becoming.

One thing I knew early was where the journey had to lead: forgiveness. Not only forgiveness of another person, but forgiveness of myself, and of the creator I had struggled to understand.

As the story grew, it moved away from the idea of an original unity and a return to perfection. I became less interested in salvation or eternal bliss than in the fragile nature of balance, and in the choices we make when balance is never guaranteed. The White Wolf is no more the whole self than The Shadow Wolf. They are two among many wolves, each carrying light and shadow in different measure.

That is the heart of this work. It is entirely fictional, except that it isn't. It is my autobiography, fabulized.

My name is Michael Stryder. I am a fabulist, and this is the world I made to tell the truth sideways.
